# Manual Enablement Runbook

OP-2 prepares but does not execute this OP-3 sequence.

1. Confirm exact deployment/version.
2. Confirm external endpoint.
3. Confirm credential validity.
4. Confirm allowlist.
5. Confirm candidate source/version.
6. Confirm metric readiness.
7. Confirm dashboard.
8. Confirm critical alert route.
9. Confirm rollback owner on standby.
10. Confirm six-role approvals.
11. Confirm effective traffic 0.
12. Approve manual 1% enablement.
13. Execute enablement.
14. Immediately inspect metrics.
15. Observe at least 30 minutes and 100 executions.
16. Exit or rollback.

Steps 12-16 are forbidden in OP-2. The manual control path and approval remain unresolved.
