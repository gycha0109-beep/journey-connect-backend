# Risk Register

Residual risks include external scrape/retention uncertainty, alert delivery uncertainty, unexecuted external rollback drills, absent empirical task-age/checkpoint thresholds, unresolved candidate integration, approval confusion and future traffic misconfiguration.

Mitigations are fail-closed local controls, explicit external readiness flags, exact-head evidence, zero-tolerance critical rules, the 1% ceiling, manual enablement and the AND-only OP-3 gate.

Production activation, candidate serving and authority transfer remain forbidden.
